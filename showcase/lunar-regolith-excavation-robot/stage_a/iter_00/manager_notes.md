
## Opening
The job divides into six functions that pass material and motion hand to hand. Traverse ground moves the whole mass over loose soil, loosen soil breaks the undisturbed bed, collect soil lifts the loosened flow into storage, contain soil holds the carried mass, discharge soil pours it into the collection bin, and coordinate blind operation turns sensor and link signals into motion and digging commands without line of sight.

Excavation and Regolith Handling Lead, Mobility and Deployable Chassis Lead, Autonomy and Blind Operations Lead, and Power Structure and Integration Lead please first post the best existing solutions from your fields with sources as concepts, then post at least one concept for every function or state your field has none, and compute what decides the design as follows: Power Structure and Integration Lead calcs the mass sum against the 60 kg limit and calcs frame deflection and simulates battery and motor heating over time, Mobility and Deployable Chassis Lead calcs the stowed sum against the 1.0 m by 0.5 m by 0.5 m box and simulates tip-over during discharge and stopping distance over time, Excavation and Regolith Handling Lead calcs Black Point 1 cut force and simulates hopper filling over time, and Autonomy and Blind Operations Lead simulates link-loss stop and blind localisation drift over time.
