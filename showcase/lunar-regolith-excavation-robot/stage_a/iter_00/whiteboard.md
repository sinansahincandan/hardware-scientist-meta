# Whiteboard
## Functions
- F1 [Mara — Excavation and Mobility Engineer] Traverse loose BP-1 bed without immobilization
- F2 [Mara — Excavation and Mobility Engineer] Excavate and lift BP-1 regolith into onboard carrying volume
- F3 [Mara — Excavation and Mobility Engineer] React digging forces while staying within 60 kg and 1.0x0.5x0.5 m limits
- F4 [Theo — Autonomy and Remote Operations Engineer] Provide remote situational awareness without line-of-sight (video, depth, orientation)
- F5 [Theo — Autonomy and Remote Operations Engineer] Maintain safe remote link with independent stop when operators cannot see or hear robot
- F6 [Priya — Power, Avionics and Lunar Environment Engineer] Supply and distribute onboard electrical power for drive, digging, computing and lighting within mass and size limits
- F7 [Priya — Power, Avionics and Lunar Environment Engineer] Protect electronics and mechanisms from BP-1 dust intrusion and thermal overload
- F8 [Sam — Structure, Mass and Material Handling Engineer] Store carried BP-1 onboard and dump into collection bin without spillage or tipping
## Concepts
- C1 [Mara — Excavation and Mobility Engineer, open, for F1] 4-wheel drive rigid wheels, 300 mm diameter x 100 mm wide, chevron grousers 15 mm tall, high ground clearance
- C2 [Mara — Excavation and Mobility Engineer, open, for F2] Front transverse bucket drum with counter-rotating scoops feeding rear hopper, like combine harvester header
- C3 [Mara — Excavation and Mobility Engineer, open, for F1] Tracked skid-steer using rubber timing-belt tracks with molded cleats, larger contact area than wheels
- C4 [Mara — Excavation and Mobility Engineer, open, for F2] Rear drag blade plus bucket ladder chain conveyor digging under chassis, like bucket-wheel excavator analogy
- C5 [Theo — Autonomy and Remote Operations Engineer, open, for F4] Elevated tilting sensor mast with dual FPV cameras, depth camera, IMU and LED lighting for out-of-sight driving and dump alignment
- C6 [Theo — Autonomy and Remote Operations Engineer, open, for F5] Dual-link teleoperation (5.8 GHz video + 2.4 GHz control) with heartbeat failsafe E-stop relay and onboard autonomy fallback to stop-and-hold
- C7 [Priya — Power, Avionics and Lunar Environment Engineer, open, for F6] 24 V Lithium Iron Phosphate battery pack (~1 kWh) with fused star distribution to drive, dig, compute and LED lighting, sized for 30 min digging sortie
- C8 [Priya — Power, Avionics and Lunar Environment Engineer, open, for F7] Sealed IP65 electronics enclosure with labyrinth shaft seals, brush skirts and positive-pressure filtered cooling fan for BP-1 dust mitigation
- C9 [Sam — Structure, Mass and Material Handling Engineer, open, for F3] Low aluminum tube space frame with belly pan: 0.95 m x 0.45 m footprint, 0.45 m tall stowed, keeps center of gravity low and carries drive plus dig loads into 60 kg budget
- C10 [Sam — Structure, Mass and Material Handling Engineer, open, for F8] Central heaped hopper with rear pivot high-lift dump: 0.50 m long hopper lifted by 150 mm stroke 500 N electric linear actuator to clear 1.0 m bin lip and pour by gravity
