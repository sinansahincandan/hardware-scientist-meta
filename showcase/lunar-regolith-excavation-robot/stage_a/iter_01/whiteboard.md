## Concepts
- C1 [Excavation and Regolith Handling Lead, open, for F2] inclined bucket-ladder digging chain with 8 buckets on chain driven by motor, plunges ~200 mm into bed — proven Lunabotics winner (Chondrobot-2 mined 8 kg BP-1 in 1 min; Alabama MARTE ladder plunged 8 in)
- C2 [Excavation and Regolith Handling Lead, favored, for F3] inclined bucket-ladder chain that both loosens and elevates soil into hopper in one motion, buckets empty over top sprocket by gravity into chute
- C3 [Excavation and Regolith Handling Lead, open, for F2] low-angle drag scraper blade (entry 20-30 deg to soil) with replaceable abrasion-resistant steel edge to shear 50 mm deep slice of BP-1, alternative to bucket ladder when traction limited
- C4 [Excavation and Regolith Handling Lead, favored, for F4] steep-wall wedge hopper (walls 60 deg above horizontal, smooth steel) 20 L volume holding about 30 kg BP-1 at compacted density, drains by gravity when gate opens
- C5 [Excavation and Regolith Handling Lead, open, for F5] rear-pivot tilting hopper dumping over bin lip with sliding gate plus vibrator to break BP-1 arching, pours through 150 mm outlet
- C6 [Excavation and Regolith Handling Lead, open, for F1] excavation has none for traverse: defers to chassis lead (notes digging reaction must stay below available traction, cut force ~33 N for 0.3 m blade at 50 mm depth)
- C7 [Excavation and Regolith Handling Lead, open, for F6] excavation has none for blind coordination: defers to autonomy lead (notes digging needs load feedback — motor current limit — and bin-alignment marker)
- C8 [Mobility and Deployable Chassis Lead, open, for F1] Tank-track chassis with counter-rotating drum arms like NASA RASSOR (Regolith Advanced Surface Systems Operations Robot) — compact tank chassis on tracks, arms lift to dump high and self-right; tracks clog with pebbles and slip off in tests
- C9 [Mobility and Deployable Chassis Lead, favored, for F1] Four-wheel independent skid-steer with rigid grouser wheels, MoonRaker heritage: 200 mm diameter plus 10 mm grousers, no suspension beyond passive differential, climbs 10 deg loose slope with slip 0.12
- C10 [Mobility and Deployable Chassis Lead, open, for F1] Folding drop-down wheel arms that stow inside 1.0 x 0.5 x 0.5 m box and deploy outward to widen track for stability before driving, unfolded after start per envelope rule
- C11 [Mobility and Deployable Chassis Lead, open, for F2] mobility has none for loosen: defers to excavation lead bucket-ladder versus scraper choice (notes traction must exceed cut force 32.9N E9)
- C12 [Mobility and Deployable Chassis Lead, open, for F3] mobility has none for collect: defers to excavation lead (notes digging chain clearance to frame and wheels must be kept)
- C13 [Mobility and Deployable Chassis Lead, open, for F4] mobility has none for contain: defers to excavation lead hopper (notes carried soil plus 60kg mass sets wheel load)
- C14 [Mobility and Deployable Chassis Lead, open, for F5] mobility has none for discharge: defers to excavation lead tilting hopper (notes tip check needs rear overhang and raised mass height)
- C15 [Mobility and Deployable Chassis Lead, open, for F6] mobility has none for blind coordination: defers to autonomy lead (notes skid-steer needs wheel odometry plus IMU for slip correction)
- C16 [Autonomy and Blind Operations Lead, favored, for F6] sealed-station camera-only teleoperation with forward drive camera plus downward dig camera plus mast view and telemetry streamed over network, operators see only onboard views — QUT Perseus heritage that teleoperates from sealed base station only via onboard cameras and sensors
- C17 [Autonomy and Blind Operations Lead, open, for F6] heartbeat watchdog link-loss stop: onboard computer halts drive and digging if command heartbeat lost for 0.5 s, relays de-energize, Latched stop until operator resumes — fail-safe e-stop product pattern with heartbeat monitoring
- C18 [Autonomy and Blind Operations Lead, open, for F6] fused blind localization: wheel odometry plus IMU extended Kalman filter with periodic camera fiducial fix on collection bin every ~5 m to bound drift, digging end defined as leading end and dump end as trailing end fixed to fiducial view
- C19 [Autonomy and Blind Operations Lead, open, for F1] autonomy has none for traverse: defers to chassis lead wheels (notes blind drive needs capped speed 0.5 m/s so watchdog stop stays under 0.35 m)
- C20 [Autonomy and Blind Operations Lead, open, for F2] autonomy has none for loosen: defers to excavation lead (notes digging needs motor-current load feedback to detect stall blind)
- C21 [Autonomy and Blind Operations Lead, open, for F3] autonomy has none for collect: defers to excavation lead (notes dig camera must see bucket emptying into hopper)
- C22 [Autonomy and Blind Operations Lead, open, for F4] autonomy has none for contain: defers to excavation lead (notes hopper level sensing by camera or load cells for blind fill stop)
- C23 [Autonomy and Blind Operations Lead, open, for F5] autonomy has none for discharge: defers to excavation lead (notes bin fiducial alignment plus mast camera confirms pour into bin)
- C24 [Power Structure and Integration Lead, open, for F1] 24 V 20 Ah LiFePO4 pack (480 Wh) on fused power-path bus to drive, ladder, tilt and compute — sized for 25 min mixed duty with margin; battery plus robot held to 47 kg against 60 kg limit
- C25 [Power Structure and Integration Lead, open, for F1] welded 6061-T6 aluminium tube chassis with bolted wheel arms, digger mounts and hopper pivots — keeps deflection to millimetres and exterior inside stowed box; assumed_constraint: working stress capped near 15 ksi per garage journal aluminium tube practice
- C26 [Power Structure and Integration Lead, open, for F2] power-structure has none for loosen: defers to excavation lead ladder versus scraper (requires rigid digger mount and current-limited ladder feed on power bus)
- C27 [Power Structure and Integration Lead, open, for F3] power-structure has none for collect: defers to excavation lead (requires chain clearance to frame and fused ladder motor branch)
- C28 [Power Structure and Integration Lead, open, for F4] power-structure has none for contain: defers to excavation lead hopper (carries 4.5 kg hopper steel plus up to 15 kg soil per trip inside 47 kg total)
- C29 [Power Structure and Integration Lead, open, for F5] power-structure has none for discharge: defers to excavation lead tilting hopper (requires rear pivot lugs rated for 15 kg soil plus hopper dead weight at full tilt)
- C30 [Power Structure and Integration Lead, open, for F6] power-structure has none for blind coordination: defers to autonomy lead (hosts compute plus cameras on 2.5 kg avionics budget with fused 5/12 V rails)
- C31 [Mobility and Deployable Chassis Lead, open, for F2] transverse milling drum loosener (distant analogy from rotary trencher/tiller): small powered drum across front milling 50 mm deep by 300 mm wide with teeth throwing loosened BP-1 back onto collection conveyor — rotary tiller breaks soil by rotation so forward draft stays small unlike drag blade; maps to low traction budget and feeds bucket-ladder or belt
- C32 [Mobility and Deployable Chassis Lead, open, for F5] high rear-pivot dump body with extended overhang (distant analogy from farm dump trailer): hopper pivots behind rear axle and lifts outlet to 0.55 m so pour clears 0.40 m bin lip with 0.15 m gap, soil slides by gravity plus vibration; maps trailer tipping rule that raised load behind axle sets tip moment to this robot rear pour
- C33 [Autonomy and Blind Operations Lead, open, for F2] autonomous current-limited dig control (distant analogy from mining excavator stall-avoidance): ladder/drum feed rate auto-reduced when motor current exceeds threshold, backs out on stall, maps excavator hoist/crowd stall avoidance to blind BP-1 digging without operator feel
- C34 [Autonomy and Blind Operations Lead, open, for F5] fiducial-guided auto-align dump (distant analogy from drone precision landing on visual pad / AGV dock): mast camera measures pose to bin AprilTag fiducial, robot creeps to aligned stance then tilts, maps landing-pad relative navigation to blind pour into bin without operator depth perception
- C35 [Power Structure and Integration Lead, open, for F2] eccentric-mass vibratory cutting edge on scraper/ladder foot (distant analogy from vibratory pile driver and concrete vibrator): small counter-rotating eccentric motor shakes cutting edge at tens of Hz to fluidize BP-1 contact so draft stays below traction on same power bus; maps pile-driver friction reduction to light robot draft limit
- C36 [Excavation and Regolith Handling Lead, favored, for F2] narrow-kerf trenching chain loosener (distant analogy from ditch-witch ride-on trencher): 100 mm wide vertical chain cuts 50 mm deep slot like a chainsaw kerf, spoil falls sideways for ladder buckets to collect — narrow kerf cuts draft to one-third of full-width blade and maps trencher spoil-row to light-robot traction limit
- C37 [Excavation and Regolith Handling Lead, favored, for F5] auger-assisted live-bottom discharge (distant analogy from grain combine unloading auger): short screw conveyor in hopper floor meters BP-1 to rear outlet during tilt so pour is controlled not slumping, maps combine metered unloading to blind pour accuracy over bin lip
