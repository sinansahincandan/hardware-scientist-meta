# Whiteboard
## Inspirations
- A1: Concept is physically plausible and COTS-buildable in kind - 4x rigid grouser wheels, front transverse drum, central hopper on rear mast, 24 V bus, sealed box, sensor mast, dual radio - no conservation, load-path or signal-path violation. RASSOR prior art does not invalidate it: single drum loses counter-rotating cancellation but 45.7 N reaction vs ~400-500 N weight gives traction ratio >5, and separate hopper + mast to 1.05 m adds tip moment and spill path but is ordinary Earth-gravity engineering, unlike RASSOR drum-as-hopper Z-stand.
Re-derivation decides the vote: 341 W x 0.5 h = 171 Wh, not 1000 Wh. 1000 Wh / 2.92 kg = 342 Wh/kg pack. Everyday scale: LiFePO4 cells ~120-140 Wh/kg, pack ~90-110 Wh/kg; laptop NMC pack ~200-250 Wh/kg cells; 342 Wh/kg LiFePO4 pack is outside everyday scale by ~3x. Either energy is ~0.17-0.3 kWh and 2.92 kg is roughly right for NMC / light LiFePO4, or energy is 1 kWh and mass should be ~8-10 kg. Even corrected, 50.6 -2.92 +~9 = ~57 kg still under 60 kg, so likely closable, but not closed. Lift energy 2400 J E60 vs 15 kg x 9.81 x 1.05 m = ~155 J useful implies 15x overhead - model, friction or time-window error to check. 10 kg at 900 kg/m3 = 0.0111 m3 E49 is correct vs 0.00625 m3 E44 error, ~11 L bucket ~ person-size carry-on, plausible. 4.06 tip factor with 0.30 m overhang vs 7.1 earlier shows moment-arm sensitivity vs wheelbarrow experience - geometry must be fixed.
Key claims resting on no evidence/source: actuator force with friction through linkage (E56 failed), runtime from pack at full load (E59 failed, E58 failed), dimensioned stowed 1.0x0.5x0.5 m envelope with 300 mm wheels + stowed 900 mm mast + 1050 mm hopper travel (sketches E11/E46/E57 show deployed topology only, E22/E45 failed), sealed-box 22 K rise E53 and 0.00332 m3/s for 80 W E52 are closed but IP65 dust claim has only generic search E27-E29, no seal/detail. Contact/torque/cutting E10, power E34, sensor power E47, hopper volume E49, stopping distance E21 have calc/simulate support.
Meeting facts: closed by cycle cap without consensus, v2 by Sam, 1 ready (Mara) to 3 not-ready (Theo, Priya, Sam). Blocking O1 mass integration, O2 dump kinematics/tip, O4 battery mass-energy all point to same two missing proofs. In my judgement those objections are sound and leave the concept unsettled - not fundamentally unworkable, but buildable not proven on sound analysis. Hence not accept yet; on resubmittal with the two recomputes above and a stowed envelope sketch, this should be accept-ready, with dust/thermal and FPV latency left as test tuning in feedback, not as acceptance blockers.
## Functions
- F1 [Mara — Excavation and Mobility Engineer] Drive over loose BP-1 simulant bed without excessive sinkage or slip, and climb out of dig pile
- F2 [Mara — Excavation and Mobility Engineer] Excavate BP-1 simulant with low cutting force and load into hopper
- F3 [Theo — Autonomy and Remote Operations Engineer] Operate with no line-of-sight and no hearing of robot via remote link with sensing, onboard compute, and heartbeat stop-and-hold
- F4 [Sam — Structure, Mass and Material Handling Engineer] Carry regolith in hopper and high-lift dump into collection bin within mass and start envelope
## Concepts
- C1 [Mara — Excavation and Mobility Engineer, open, for F1] Locomotion: four rigid wheels with grousers (traction bars), 300 mm diameter x 150 mm wide. Analogy: farm tractor and Mars rover wheels - large diameter spreads weight, grousers bite loose soil.
- C2 [Mara — Excavation and Mobility Engineer, open, for F2] Excavator: front transverse rotating drum (bucket-wheel style, RASSOR analogy) 200 mm dia x 400 mm wide, shallow cut 30 mm, lifts soil into central hopper. Alternative: drag blade. Drum chosen for continuous cut and low shock.
- C3 [Theo — Autonomy and Remote Operations Engineer, open, for F3] Folding perception mast: two First Person View (FPV) cameras plus depth sensor and Inertial Measurement Unit (IMU) on folding mast, stowed flat under 0.5 m, deployed to ~1.0 m for view over hopper and drum. Analogy: sailboat mast and periscope - low stow, high eye.
- C4 [Theo — Autonomy and Remote Operations Engineer, open, for F3] Heartbeat stop-and-hold teleoperation: 5 GHz WiFi link with heartbeat monitor, onboard compute hold-to-run autonomy, Emergency Stop (E-stop) latching stop-and-hold on link loss or latency exceedance. Analogy: industrial Automated Guided Vehicle (AGV) deadman switch.
- C5 [Sam — Structure, Mass and Material Handling Engineer, open, for F4] Rear high-lift mast hopper dump: hopper pivot 0.45 m stowed rising to 1.05 m pour lip (0.60 m rise) via 0.15 m stroke dual actuators through 4-to-1 chain at 0.80 friction efficiency. Analogy forklift mast.
