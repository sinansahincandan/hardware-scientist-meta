Hardware Scientist · Meta Global AI Developer Hackathon

From a one-paragraph problem to a hardware concept that works

Early concept design decides most of a device's cost and failure. One AI prompt returns a confident picture with no sources, no calculations and no check that it works.

Hardware Scientist is a self-improving AI design team: experts search, calculate and sketch on a shared whiteboard, a chief designer decides, an Evaluator checks that it works, and every critique retrains the team.

The whole input
Infant warmer for clinics without reliable power. Keeps the baby at about 37 °C for at least 4 hours without electricity; costs at most 100 US dollars.
The output: wax heat pouches charged in hot water hold the baby at 36.8 °C for 6 hours, for about $50.
