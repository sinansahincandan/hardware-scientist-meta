Closest existing product:

**DEICON - Underneath pedestrian-bridge TMDs, 2x units, 500 kg combined at midspan.**

* What it is:
  * `TMDs are specialized tuned damping devices` with `mass suspended by restoring element and damping device` for `abating vibration at a particular natural frequency`.
  * `Figure 1 TMDs installed underneath, near the edge of, the balcony`.
  * `DEICON designed and fabricated two tuned mass dampers with a combined moving mass of 500 kg`.
  * `dampers were installed at the bridge midspan adjacent to the two main girders`.
  * `Integrating the units within the bridge structure allowed the vibration-control system to remain concealed while maintaining direct influence over the dominant modal responses` - note citation id correction: same page `Figure 1 shows one of the TMDs being lowered into its housing cavity` + `concealed while maintaining direct influence`.
  * Validated by `Acceleration data` with `TMDs locked (inactive) and then repeated with the TMDs unlocked and operational`, showing `effective damping increased by nearly a factor of 3 for the 1.1 Hz mode and nearly doubling for the 2.8 Hz mode`.

Difference to this concept `Concealed Under-Deck Tuned Mass Damper for 40 m Footbridge Vertical Comfort`:

1.  Single-mode vs dual-mode tuning: Concept is single first-vertical-bending `1.9 Hz` system tuned to `1.86 Hz` with `0.086 TMD damping ratio` for `~2% mass ratio` / `400 kg`. DEICON targets `two dominant vibration modes: a vertical bending mode and a torsional mode with closely spaced natural frequencies`, specifically `dominant modes near 1.1 Hz and 2.4 Hz, corresponding to the bridge's primary bending and twisting modes`, and `tuned to an intermediate frequency between the two, allowing a single implementation to provide damping to both vibration modes simultaneously`. Concept uses Den Hartog optimum for one mode, not intermediate compromise.

2.  Mounting / concealment detail: Concept specifies `bolted cross-frames between girders`, `30 mm clearance`, `no deck penetration`, `below-deck hatch access` with two `200 kg` modules. DEICON is `installed underneath, near the edge` `adjacent to the two main girders` lowered into `its housing cavity` - edge housings for bending + torsion control, not centre soffit bolted cross-frame for maximum midspan bending motion with explicit stroke / re-tuning provision.

3.  Mass / scale: `500 kg combined` vs concept `400 kg` as `two 200 kg` for 40 m / 1.9 Hz bridge to meet `0.5 m/s2 maximum-comfort limit`.

Other near neighbours, more distant: Millennium Bridge `26 more pairs of vertically acting absorbers were distributed across the complete span, all out of sight under the bridge deck` - distributed multi-unit approach, not 2-unit midspan; generic MAURER `MTMD-V, MTMD-H` and GERB `frequency range 0.1 Hz - 200 Hz and vibration mass 500 g - 600 t` catalogue TMDs - not footbridge-specific concealed under-deck embodiment.
