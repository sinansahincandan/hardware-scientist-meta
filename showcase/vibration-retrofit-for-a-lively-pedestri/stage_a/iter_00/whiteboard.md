## Concepts
- C1 [Elena - Structural Dynamics Engineer, open, for F1] assumed_constraint: vertical comfort target maximum comfort below 0.5 m/s2, minimum comfort below 1.0 m/s2, unacceptable above 2.5 m/s2 per Setra and HiVoSS comfort classes
- C2 [Marcus - Vibration Control and Damping Specialist, open, for F2] Under-deck vertical Tuned Mass Damper (TMD): lumped steel mass on helical steel springs plus viscous dashpots, tuned to first vertical mode, mounted at midspan under deck so deck appearance unchanged
- C3 [Priya - Retrofit Constructability Engineer, open, for F3] Bolted under-deck support frame: two transverse steel bearer beams clamped with preloaded high-strength bolts to existing longitudinal girders, carrying the 500 kg TMD mass on springs plus dashpots; no welding to bridge, no change above deck
- C4 [Priya - Retrofit Constructability Engineer, open, for F3] assumed_constraint: staged night installation plus under-bridge access: prefabricate frame plus TMD off site, lift from tower plus under-bridge inspection platform with one lane kept open, lock mass for transport and release after bolting, leave removable cover plus shim and threaded spring seats for inspection and re-tuning
- C5 [Tomas - Pedestrian Comfort and Safety Analyst, open, for F4] assumed_constraint: dense-crowd verification case Class III 0.5 pedestrians per square metre, equivalent synchronized pedestrians from Setra 10.8*sqrt(zeta*N), single-pedestrian weight 700 N with 280 N first-harmonic amplitude at resonance
- C6 [Tomas - Pedestrian Comfort and Safety Analyst, open, for F4] comfort acceptance: maximum comfort below 0.5 m/s2 and minimum comfort below 1.0 m/s2 per Setra and HiVoSS, 1.9 Hz lies inside 1.25 to 2.3 Hz critical range so resonant crowd case governs
