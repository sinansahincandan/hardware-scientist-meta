## Concepts
- C1 [Structural Dynamicist, open, for F2] Tuned mass absorber tuned to 1.9 Hz — sprung steel mass under deck supplying inertial counteracting force (Millennium Bridge 26 pairs vertical absorbers 1-3 t, mass ratio 3-5% per Cambridge ICSV review)
- C2 [Structural Dynamicist, open, for F1] Under-deck vertical coil-spring plus linear guide suspension that converts bridge vertical motion into large relative mass travel (Millennium vertical tuned absorbers on transoms per Cambridge ICSV review)
- C3 [Structural Dynamicist, open, for F3] Viscous dashpot in TMD tuned to Den Hartog optimum damping ratio sqrt(3 mu/8(1+mu)) — converts relative motion to heat at small amplitudes
- C4 [Structural Dynamicist, open, for F4] Steel bracket frame bolting TMD units to main girders at midspan antinode, carrying inertia plus static weight into primary structure
- C5 [Structural Dynamicist, open, for F5] Elastomeric end stops plus caged travel gap sized beyond design TMD stroke to catch over-travel during crowd surge or detuning
- C6 [Structural Dynamicist, open, for F6] Conceal all TMD hardware below deck soffit attached to girders leaving deck sightline and walkway untouched (Millennium constraint: appearance unchanged per Cambridge review, mods out of sight under deck)
- C7 [Bridge Retrofit Engineer, open, for F2] Added under-deck stiffness alternative — paired longitudinal stiffener ribs plus transverse diaphragms under soffit raising bending frequency out of walking band, versus pure added damping (stiffening-vs-damping tradeoff for 40 m span)
- C8 [Bridge Retrofit Engineer, open, for F4] HSFG-clamped under-deck bracket frame — preloaded High-Strength Friction-Grip bolts clamping TMD and damper reactions into main girders at midspan antinode without welding, sized for inertia plus static weight; assumed_constraint: total added dead load under 2% of pedestrian live load
