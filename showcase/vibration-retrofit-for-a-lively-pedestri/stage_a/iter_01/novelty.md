Twin under-deck damper hunt is on — I'll find its closest built match.
I've narrowed it to a couple of strong twin-damper candidates — now I'll dig into the details.
Closest match: **MAURER — Retrofit twin TMDs under Zinggen Bridge, Brixen / South Tyrol** — commercial product + site installation report.

Product:

* 36.2 m single-span steel trough bike/pedestrian bridge over Eisack, welded construction with vertical vibrations.
* Requirement: `no intrusions into the structure` , MAURER scope from model calculation to design, production and assembly.
* HIVOSS pedestrian scenarios simulated to set max accelerations, e.g. `0.35 m/s2 for a runner`.
* Classic TMD: `mass rests on four coil springs`, springs adjusted to swing vertically off-phase “against” eigenfrequency of structure.
* Verified by `acceleration sensors were placed onto the bridge` and test pedestrians/groups walked/ran across, manufactured to `frequency 2.45 Hz and swinging mass 480 kg`.
* Each unit `length 1.350 mm, height 400 mm, width 250 mm` with `frame protecting the springs and the mass` installed as stable package underneath bridge.

Critical split that makes it closest to concept:

> single calculated damper could not fit underneath due to longitudinal stabilizing ribs, spacings too small, therefore `required damper mass was divided into two small dampers with identical construction, each featuring half the mass` fitted into rib spacings `so that they are not visible from outside`.

### Difference vs. proposed concept

Twin 400 kg x 2 = 800 kg hanging on HSFG-clamped cross-beam, tuned ~1.83 Hz for 1.9 Hz 40 m bridge, coil-spring + separate viscous damper, stops, below-deck service + acceptance run to 0.67 m/s2 Medium:

1.  **Support embodiment:** Concept uses **one** 2.5-3.0 m transverse steel cross-beam carrying **both** units, clamped between girder webs by HSFG M20 10.9 bolts — 32.9 kN slip/bolt vs 13.1 kN demand incl. stop hit. MAURER uses **two separate packaged frame units** inserted in rib bays, no transverse load-sharing beam disclosed.
2.  **Tuning philosophy:** Concept is **detuned MTMD pair about 1.83 Hz** for robustness 0.476-0.526 m/s2 tolerant band + 6.2% effective damping. MAURER pair is `identical construction, each half mass` tuned to single 2.45 Hz target.
3.  **Suspension/dissipation:** Concept separates `coil spring suspension` + `paired viscous dampers` as hanging masses with 49.4 mm stroke in 80 mm gap, 74.7 mm sag / 124 mm envelope, caged elastomer stops 8.4 kN hit. MAURER discloses only `mass rests on four coil springs` + protective frame — no stop gap / sag+stroke / separate viscous unit disclosed.
4.  **Verification target:** Concept targets Class III crowd 8.28 -> 0.67 m/s2 Medium with midspan accelerometer -> comfort-band verdict record. MAURER targets HIVOSS incl. runner 0.35 m/s2 via pre-manufacture on-site measurement to avoid readjustment, not a post-build Class III crowd acceptance filing.

Second-close for reference: **DEICON pedestrian bridge twin TMDs, 500 kg combined, installed at midspan adjacent to two main girders** **underneath near edge**, but tuned to **intermediate frequency** to damp bending + torsion simultaneously — not a single-mode detuned vertical pair on common HSFG cross-beam with stops.
