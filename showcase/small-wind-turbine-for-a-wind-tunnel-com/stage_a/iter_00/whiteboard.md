## Objections
- O1 (resolved) Parked hold 0.400 N·m is below stalled aero torque 0.480 N·m in E48 and below 0.720 N·m with factor 1.5 in E54, so disc alone cannot guarantee parked stay at 22 m/s without soft-stall reduction or larger pad radius.
- O2 (resolved) Regulated feed drops at 5 m/s: low-wind volts 6.50 V (E37) is below 8 V minimum of Buck/Boost Converter (E60), so C26 with 220 Kv cannot produce regulated power across 5-11 m/s. Fix by 170-180 Kv giving 8.41 V low and 18.5 V top (E76) within 8-40 V window.
## Chief designer's selections
