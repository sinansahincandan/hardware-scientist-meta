
## Opening
Six functions do the whole touch job from skin to hand controller. Conform contact surface takes in touch energy and gives a pressure field, support fingertip load carries that load into the finger, transduce contact stress turns local pressure into electrical signals, resolve contact location turns the array signals into where and how hard, detect slip onset watches change over time to warn of slip, and transmit tactile message sends the digital touch message to the hand.
