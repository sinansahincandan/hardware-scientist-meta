# Whiteboard
## Functions
- F1 [Maya — Tactile Transduction and Sensing Physics] Localize contact on fingertip surface
- F2 [Maya — Tactile Transduction and Sensing Physics] Measure normal and shear contact force from 1 mN to grip force
- F3 [Maya — Tactile Transduction and Sensing Physics] Detect slip onset and vibration/texture
- F4 [Sam — Robot Hand Integration and Manipulation] Mount fingertip to robot finger and route wiring while surviving grip loads
## Concepts
- C1 [Maya — Tactile Transduction and Sensing Physics, open, for F2] Capacitive normal+shear taxel array: comb electrodes on PCB with silicone pillar dielectric, taxel pitch ~2.5 mm; static force channel. Abbreviation: PCB (printed circuit board).
- C2 [Maya — Tactile Transduction and Sensing Physics, open, for F3] Piezoelectric polymer film (PVDF, polyvinylidene fluoride) overlay for high-frequency vibration/slip transients; complements static channel like Pacinian corpuscle. Abbreviation: PVDF (polyvinylidene fluoride).
- C3 [Maya — Tactile Transduction and Sensing Physics, open, for F1] assumed_constraint: fingertip contact area ~15 mm x 20 mm with 2.5 mm taxel pitch gives ~48 taxels, matching human 2-8 mm two-point threshold; grip force range to ~30 N from hand datasheets.
- C4 [Leo — Soft Materials, Skin Mechanics and Fabrication, open, for F2] C4 compliant skin stack: 1.5-3 mm silicone outer skin Shore A 30 over molded pillar dielectric on rigid core; assumed_constraint fingertip hemisphere 15-20 mm diameter mountable cap. Abbreviation: LSR (liquid silicone rubber).
- C5 [Priya — Embedded Electronics, Signal Processing and Slip Detection, open, for F2] C5 multiplexed dual-rate readout: 48 capacitive taxels in 6x8 row-column mux to one CDC sampling static force at 25 Hz, plus separate PVDF vibration channel through charge amp to fast ADC at 2 kHz, fused in fingertip MCU to single 4-wire bus. Abbreviations: CDC (capacitance-to-digital converter), MCU (microcontroller unit), ADC (analog-to-digital converter), I2C (inter-integrated circuit bus).
- C6 [Sam — Robot Hand Integration and Manipulation, open, for F4] stiff quick-change fingertip cartridge: rigid core on fingertip holder with 2x M3 screws, rear-exit 4-wire flex tail, skin as replaceable cap. Abbreviations: M3 (3 mm metric screw), NBR (nitrile rubber).

## Objections
- O1 [Manager -> proposal, blocking=True] Two base capacitances (0.111 pF vs 0.332 pF) and two stiffnesses (1 MPa solid vs 20 kPa effective pillars) give conflicting 1 mN margins (0.053 fF vs 2.66 fF); reconcile to one stiffness and one delta-C vs parasitics/noise, plus missing mass sum and calibration flow, before vote.
## Proposal
- v1 [Sam — Robot Hand Integration and Manipulation] Integrated fingertip cartridge: rigid core with 2x M3 holder screws carries PCB with capacitive taxels plus PVDF vibration film under silicone pillar dielectric and outer skin, readout by row-column mux to MCU to rear 4-wire bus. Radial stack PCB 1.6 mm plus pillars 1.0 mm plus PVDF 0.11 mm plus skin 1.5 mm equals 4.21 mm, fits in 15-20 mm diameter hemispherical cap with 7 mm core for screws. Single design load 30 N nominal grip (100 kPa over 300 mm2); 139 N Hand-E limit is survival proof only; 14.8 N for 1 kg at 5 m/s2 is normal operating point inside envelope.
