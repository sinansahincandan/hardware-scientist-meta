- F2 [Manager] Support fingertip load (flows: In: robot finger structure material, reaction load from contact. Out: fixed fingertip body material, reaction force into finger.)
  concepts from: Dr. Amara Okafor — Soft Skin Mechanics, Dr. Elena Vasquez — Contact Dynamics and Slip Detection, Dr. Marcus Reid — Robotic Integration and Embedded Electronics, Prof. Kenji Tanaka — Micro-Transduction Arrays

  concepts from: Dr. Amara Okafor — Soft Skin Mechanics, Dr. Elena Vasquez — Contact Dynamics and Slip Detection, Dr. Marcus Reid — Robotic Integration and Embedded Electronics, Prof. Kenji Tanaka — Micro-Transduction Arrays
