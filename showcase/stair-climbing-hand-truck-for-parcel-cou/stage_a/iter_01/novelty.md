### Difference of v4 concept vs prior

1. **Brake logic inverted + positive lock:**
Boyd is **squeeze-to-brake** band: grip squeezed moves arms `from the full to broken line positions` `` to slow.
No spring-applied, no hands-off hold, no tooth pin.
Concept is **release-to-lock dead-man**: `spring-applied disc plus tooth pin holds slope when lever is released` — hands-off 74.6 N m hold. Boyd must be actively held; if courier lets go on slope, Boyd free-wheels.

2. **Disc + tooth park pin vs band-only:**
Boyd: `bands will normally remain spaced from the periphery of disc 40` `` — friction only.
Concept adds disc **plus tooth lock** C7/C20 for positive engagement under full slope load. Prior art Soto-type control of star rotation noted only as `star plate arrangement with means for controlling the rotation` ``, not spring dead-man + shear pin.

3. **Structure for 60 kg + stow:**
SY395682 is `Fully welded` fixed 1180 x 480 x 600 mm `` with `Footiron W x D 300 x 245mm` `` — no fold, no brake.
Folding commercial trucks fold toe/handle/wheels but have **no slope brake/lock**.
Concept adds: one-piece twin-tube 40x36 mm backbone to cut whip to 43.9 mm, folding 365x240 mm toe, sliding handle, clip-on brace for `1150 x 380 x 190 mm` diagonal van stow.

In short: **Boyd + SY395682 = tri-star + band brake OR 60 kg tri-star + skids. Neither = tri-star + dead-man spring disc + positive tooth hands-off lock + folding twin-tube backbone.**
