# Whiteboard
## Functions
- F1 [Maya – Stair-Climbing Mechanisms Engineer] Move 60 kg load up and down residential stairs on one-person power
- F2 [Maya – Stair-Climbing Mechanisms Engineer] Hold load and control descent on stair nosing without runaway
- F3 [Maya – Stair-Climbing Mechanisms Engineer] Carry 60 kg load path through frame to toe plate and handles with safety factor
- F4 [Jonas – Ergonomics and Courier Safety Specialist] Provide one-person grip, posture and load retention with fail-safe descent control
- F5 [Priya – Lightweight Foldable Structures Engineer] Fold flat into van envelope and keep tare liftable by one courier while locking rigid when unfolded
## Concepts
- C1 [Maya – Stair-Climbing Mechanisms Engineer, open, for F1] tri-star wheel cluster: 3x 150 mm wheels per side on rotating carrier, orbit radius ~140 mm to step over 200 mm riser
- C2 [Jonas – Ergonomics and Courier Safety Specialist, open, for F4] [assumed_constraint] One-person handle effort limit: sustained pull below 250 N, one-step heave below 350 N; handles adjustable 90-110 cm height, closed-loop grips. Based on ISO 11228-2 push-pull guidance (E7) and HSE push-pull report (E8).
- C3 [Jonas – Ergonomics and Courier Safety Specialist, open, for F2] Descent control: friction brake band on tri-star carrier plus nosing stop pawl, deadman lever on handle, fail-safe locked when released
- C4 [Jonas – Ergonomics and Courier Safety Specialist, open, for F4] Load retention: two-point harness strap over parcel to toe plate with buckle plus heel stop, anti-slip toe plate, pinch guards at tri-star wheel cluster (C1)
- C5 [Priya – Lightweight Foldable Structures Engineer, open, for F5] Mid-spine single fold hinge + flip-up toe plate: two 6061-T6 (heat-treated aluminium alloy) 30x2mm spine tubes unfold to 1200mm straight spine, over-centre side plates with spring detent pin and green-red lock indicator; folds to flat slab.
- C6 [Priya – Lightweight Foldable Structures Engineer, open, for F5] [assumed_constraint] Folded envelope under 750x550x150mm and tare under 12kg liftable: targets proven flat-truck class 490x55x730mm folded niche fit and 250kg-capable folders folding to 120mm thick.
- C7 [Leo – Last-Mile Operations and Van Integration Specialist, open, for F5] [assumed_constraint] Van parcel interface: toe plate 450x350mm holds 40x30x30 to 60x40x40cm parcels with strap; folded slab 730x490x150mm max stows upright in van niche; single-hinge unfold with flip toe under 30s; smooth wipe-clean aluminium and solid tyres.
- C8 [Maya – Stair-Climbing Mechanisms Engineer, open, for F3] chassis load path: twin 6061-T6 spine tubes to 5mm toe plate 450x350mm, 60kg on 200mm overhang, bending via spine to tri-star carrier axle, safety factor >2 on yield
