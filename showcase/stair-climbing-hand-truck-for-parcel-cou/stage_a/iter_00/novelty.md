**Closest prior art: SterraClimb LLC – US20090133517A1**
`Mechanical Tri-Wheel Retention Assembly for Stair-Climbing Wheeled Vehicle` – Abandoned US Appl. 12/254,854, pub. 2009-05-28.

Why closest:

* Generic stair-climber sack-trucks use `tri-star or triple-wheel cluster on each side` and optional `folding model` for van storage.
* Only this patent family combines tri-wheel / spider + clutch + brake hold on stairs like concept:
  > `pair of spider assemblies rotatably supported` ... `plurality of rotatable wheels coupled to rotate in synchronicity`
  > `vehicle may include a variable engagement clutch and brake system`
  > `clutch can either lock the wheels to the same reference frame as the hand truck frame, or can allow them to spin freely`
  > `clutch system is essential for providing added driving traction to force the hand truck to climb the stairs, rather than roll off or bounce in place`
  > `clutch also can act as a brake to lock the hand truck to the stairs, reducing the possibility that it would roll off if the user were to stop at some point during ascent or descent`

**Difference to proposed concept:**

1.  Powered / active vs passive fixed + deadman metered:
Patent is `electrically-powered, driven-spider` with `microprocessor-controlled fixed-spider mode`, with `electric motor and a power source ... operatively connected to drive the pair of spider assemblies` and `controller ... to cause the electric motor to apply varying rotational torque ... as a function of input received from the angular position sensor`.
Concept: purely mechanical `FIXED twin R95` stars, `CSK30 110Nm` one-way hold `38.5Nm/405N` + `R60 deadman disc` meter to `40.5N`, no motor, no sensor, no microprocessor.

2.  Clutch type:
Patent: `clutch is electromagnetic and fully controlled by the controller; no user control is required` + tri-lobular roller cam + spring-loaded roller pop-out on overload + solenoid disengage.
Concept: COTS `CSK30 30x62x16` sprag one-way `SF2.86` + separate friction disc `R60 deadman`, sealed `2RS` for wet/gritty, manual deadman metering, quantified `60s no-slip` + `no >150N spike`.

3.  Structure / fold / stow:
Patent: `rigid frame supporting a rotatable axle`, `removable cargo baskets, and a dual-platform load-carrying system`, `telescoping, rotatable handle`, `wheel-guarding enclosures` same.
Concept: `SINGLE vertical 60x40x4 6061-T6` spine carrying `M317.8Nm`, `HAZ 25mm off peak`, only `H1 handle + H2 toe fold in 26s to 850x550x260 standing on toe+stars`, `420mm track / 550mm width` for `762/726mm doors`, `1392mm between arches`, `DESTACO 207-USS` latch for `1.04Grms` van – none disclosed in patent.
