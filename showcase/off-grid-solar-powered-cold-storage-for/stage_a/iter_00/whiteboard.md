## Concepts
- C1 [Surya — Off-Grid Solar Power Specialist, open, for F1] Fixed-tilt monocrystalline PV array: 6 x 400 W panels (~2.4 kWp) in 2 strings, tilt = latitude facing equator, on ground/roof rack behind cold room
- C2 [Surya — Off-Grid Solar Power Specialist, open, for F1] Battery plus controller cabinet: 48 V 100 Ah LiFePO4 battery (~5 kWh) with 60 A MPPT charge controller, ventilated shaded cabinet beside cold room — assumed_constraint: sized for one full night plus cloudy-day margin
- C3 [Surya — Off-Grid Solar Power Specialist, open, for F1] Low-loss Direct Current wiring: panels in series pairs to raise voltage and cut current, short thick copper cables in conduit, fuses and disconnect at cabinet
- C4 [Lena — Refrigeration and Thermal Insulation Engineer, open, for F2] 100 mm Polyurethane sandwich-panel cold box: 2 x 2 x 2.2 m inside, cam-lock panels, sealed door with gaskets, shaded outside — assumed_constraint: 100 mm thickness for 30 K hot ambient
- C5 [Lena — Refrigeration and Thermal Insulation Engineer, open, for F2] Direct Current vapor-compression chiller: 48 V Direct Current compressor with Coefficient of Performance about 2.5, wall evaporator plus shaded condenser, thermostat 4-10 C
- C6 [Lena — Refrigeration and Thermal Insulation Engineer, open, for F2] Uniform low-velocity airflow: raised slatted floor, stacked vented crates with gaps, small fan blowing over evaporator for even temperature
- C7 [Farah — Post-Harvest and Cooperative Operations Specialist, open, for F3] Stackable vented food-grade crates 600x400x300 mm, about 60 crates each holding 15-20 kg to total 1 tonne, cross-stacked with vent gaps
- C8 [Farah — Post-Harvest and Cooperative Operations Specialist, open, for F3] Two low side racks with centre aisle: crates stacked 3 high on slatted platforms, 100 mm wall gap and floor lift for air and cleaning, single 900 mm hinged door — assumed_constraint: racking fits 2x2 m box with human reach under 1.8 m
- C9 [Farah — Post-Harvest and Cooperative Operations Specialist, open, for F3] Washable hygienic use routine: smooth metal liner walls, coved washable floor, first-in-first-out crate rotation labels, daily mop and weekly crate wash at shaded wash point
- C10 [Kofi — Structural Enclosure and Low-Cost Fabrication Specialist, open, for F4] Raised reinforced concrete plinth 2.6x2.6x0.15 m with damp-proof membrane and edge upstand, washable coved floor, anchors for panel frame and racks
- C11 [Kofi — Structural Enclosure and Low-Cost Fabrication Specialist, open, for F4] Bolted galvanized steel angle support frame inside PU sandwich panels, panels as stressed skin, PV ground rack separate to avoid roof loads
- C12 [Kofi — Structural Enclosure and Low-Cost Fabrication Specialist, open, for F4] Single 900 mm insulated hinged door with cam-lock seals, 3 heavy hinges plus lever latch and padlock hasp, shaded canopy over door and condenser
