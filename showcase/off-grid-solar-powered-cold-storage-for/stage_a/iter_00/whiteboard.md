# Whiteboard
## Functions
- F1 [Amara - Off-Grid Solar Power Lead] Provide solar-only electrical power day and night for 1 tonne cold storage
- F2 [Baraka - Refrigeration and Thermal Engineer] Maintain 1 tonne produce at chill temperature with insulation, refrigeration and air circulation
- F3 [Chidi - Enclosure and Structural Designer] Provide weatherproof insulated chamber, load-bearing frame, door and 1 tonne crate layout that is transportable and cooperatively assemblable
## Concepts
- C1 [Amara - Off-Grid Solar Power Lead, open, for F1] 4x550W monocrystalline array (~2.2kW) tilted for hot-region sun
- C2 [Amara - Off-Grid Solar Power Lead, open, for F1] 48V 100Ah LiFePO4 bank (5.12kWh) plus MPPT 150V 60A controller for overnight autonomy
- C3 [Amara - Off-Grid Solar Power Lead, open, for F1] assumed_constraint: fixed tilt ~ latitude facing equator, ground mount with airflow gap for cooling
- C4 [Baraka - Refrigeration and Thermal Engineer, open, for F2] 100mm polyurethane sandwich insulation giving U about 0.22 W/m2K for walls roof floor
- C5 [Baraka - Refrigeration and Thermal Engineer, open, for F2] DC vapor-compression refrigeration at ~300W cooling, COP about 2, fan-forced evaporator for uniform chill
- C6 [Baraka - Refrigeration and Thermal Engineer, open, for F2] assumed_constraint: hold 5 degC and 85-95 percent Relative Humidity, where Relative Humidity means moist air fraction, avoiding chilling injury below tropical range
- C7 [Chidi - Enclosure and Structural Designer, open, for F3] Flat-pack cam-lock insulated box: 100mm Polyurethane sandwich panels 960mm wide, internal about 2.4x2.0x2.2m high, hinged 800mm insulated door with magnetic gaskets and outer rain canopy
- C8 [Chidi - Enclosure and Structural Designer, open, for F3] Load-bearing base and roof frame: raised galvanized steel skid with floor panels on top, bolted corner posts and roof rails carrying panel array tilted for drainage, ground anchors for wind
- C9 [Chidi - Enclosure and Structural Designer, open, for F3] Crate and rack layout for 1 tonne: two-tier slatted racks along walls with central aisle, 600x400mm ventilated produce crates stacked 5 high with air gaps, floor load spread by plywood spreaders
- C10 [Dalia - Cooperative Use and Produce Preservation Specialist, open, for F3] 20kg ventilated food-grade crate workflow: 50 crates for 1 tonne, 2 side racks with central aisle, sorting outside door, first-in first-out rotation by date labels
- C11 [Dalia - Cooperative Use and Produce Preservation Specialist, open, for F2] Spoilage prevention practice: pre-sort to remove damaged diseased produce, avoid bruising, keep crates vented with air gaps, daily check and remove spoiled items
- C12 [Dalia - Cooperative Use and Produce Preservation Specialist, open, for F3] assumed_constraint: washable food-safe interior with drain and 20kg single-person lift limit for safe cooperative handling and cleaning
- C13 [Baraka - Refrigeration and Thermal Engineer, open, for F2] Variable-speed DC vapor-compression refrigeration ~1.0 kW cooling, COP ~2, fan-forced evaporator for uniform chill plus condensate drain, sized for staged daily pull-down plus wall leak
