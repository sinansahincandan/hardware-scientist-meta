# Rationale — Off-grid 1-tonne solar cold store v3

Provide off-grid solar cold storage for 1 tonne produce holding 5 C at 43 C ambient with night autonomy and safe standing against wind and soft soil.

## Risks
- Cloud, extra openings or full-tonne load breaks 3.10 kWh margin and autonomy — mitigation: Enforce staged 250 kg per day, 8 openings, strip curtain, refusal of 51st crate and full warm load.
- Hot-ambient derate and non-uniform rear-to-front airflow fails pull-down — mitigation: Use R134a serviceable monoblock with shaded condenser, sealed penetration, trapped drain and 43 C bench test.
- Wind uplift 673 kg overcomes anchorage on soft soil — mitigation: Separate chamber and ground-frame footings with eight screw anchors and field pull test.

## Novelty
Narrower 1-tonne staged 3.60 kWp split-array PIR-box instantiation, not a new principle
