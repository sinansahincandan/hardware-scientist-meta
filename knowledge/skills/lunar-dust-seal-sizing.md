1. Characterize simulant: BP-1 particle size distribution and abrasiveness as dusty, angular fines requiring IP5X class sealing (E32, E33, E42, E43).
2. Identify ingress paths: wheel bearings, hopper doors/dust curtains, chassis hinges, battery bay and mast joints.
3. Select seal architecture: labyrinth seals + dust curtains + sealed aluminium under-belly bay with finned heat spreader for conduction without airflow (P6, E32).
4. Check verify: IP5X dust ingress, Hardox wear plates on scraper/hopper (P3,P4) surviving 10-dump dust exposure without lip-seal jamming (open question).
5. Typical values: BP-1 bulk density 1.55 g/cm3 (E45,E56); IP5X target for BP-1 fines (E32); sealed bay required for 480 Wh LiFePO4 in dusty field (E36,E37).
