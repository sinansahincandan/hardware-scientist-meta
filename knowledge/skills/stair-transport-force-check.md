## 1. Capture stair geometry
1. Measure riser, going and effective slope on the served stairs. Plan for residential band **riser 180-200 mm, going 250-280 mm, slope ~35-39 deg** with ~200 mm vertical nosing every ~250 mm tread [run 20261102-105608_stair-climbing-hand-truck-for-parcel-cou E1,E2,E3,E58].
2. Compute slope-parallel weight `Fslope = W*sin(theta)`. Example check: **W=706.3 N, theta=38.7 deg -> Fslope=441 N** [run 20261102-105608_stair-climbing-hand-truck-for-parcel-cou E58].

## 2. Check against one-person push-pull envelope
3. Compare continuous drag to **ISO 11228-2 / HSE sustained 200-250 N, brief heave <~350 N, closed-loop grips at ~90-110 cm height** [run 20261102-105608_stair-climbing-hand-truck-for-parcel-cou E7,E8,E12].
4. If `Fslope` exceeds envelope (e.g. 441 N > 250 N), require stepwise levered lift or powered assist. Reference levered class **Flever=226 N** for the 60-kg stair case [run 20261102-105608_stair-climbing-hand-truck-for-parcel-cou E58].

## 3. Budget energy and fail-safe hold
5. Budget potential energy per riser `E=m*g*h`: **116-141 J per 0.2 m riser for 60 kg** [run 20261102-105608_stair-climbing-hand-truck-for-parcel-cou E19,E58].
6. Size descent brake for nosing contact **holding ~90-100 N m (92.5-98.9 N m example), apply factor 2 -> ~198 N m** for control [run 20261102-105608_stair-climbing-hand-truck-for-parcel-cou E50,E58].
7. Require **positive locking + load restraint with default-locked brake on release** per EN 1757 industrial-truck practice to prevent runaway on incline [run 20261102-105608_stair-climbing-hand-truck-for-parcel-cou E15,E36].

## 4. Verify van stow and load interface
8. Fold to **slab <~750x550x150 mm** that stows upright in Transit/Sprinter sliding-door opening and **unfolds in <~30 s** with wipe-clean aluminium and puncture-free tyres [run 20261102-105608_stair-climbing-hand-truck-for-parcel-cou E33,E34,E35,E43,E46].
9. Size toe plate **~450x350 mm to span ~40x30x30 cm to ~60x40x40 cm parcels** [run 20261102-105608_stair-climbing-hand-truck-for-parcel-cou E49].
