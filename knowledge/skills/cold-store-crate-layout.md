1. Compute gross interior LxWxH -> net after gaps: e.g. 2.5x1.6x2.0 m =8.0 m3 gross E39, ~5.0 m3 net after 300 mm wall / 450 mm ceiling gaps E41, E67.
2. Apply FAO airflow clearances: >=300 mm wall return gap, 100-150 mm floor, 450 mm ceiling, 0.8 m central cold aisle for evaporator blow E41, E67.
3. Size crate envelope: 60x40x30 cm =72 L vented PP crate E42, E67; 20 crates =1.44 m3 envelope.
4. Check mass capacity: bulk density ~0.60 t/m3 tomato -> 1.44 m3 x 0.60 =1.0 t within net volume E39; allow 2-tier x 0.6 m deep racks each side E67.
5. Verify access: 0.9x1.8 m gasket door + PVC strip/self-closer, aisle maintains door swing and stack height E67, E56.
6. Recompute floor load: produce 9.81 kN E37 + enclosure/skid/racks ~818 kg -> total ~1818 kg E61 on 4-5 m2 footprint -> 3.07-4.27 kPa E38/E60/E66 vs XPS 300 kPa E58 creep limit.
