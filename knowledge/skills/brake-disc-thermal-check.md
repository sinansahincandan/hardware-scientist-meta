name: brake-disc-thermal-check
description: Use when checking a small high-speed rotor disc brake for temperature rise from spin-down energy.
source: run 20261104-121014_small-wind-turbine-for-a-wind-tunnel-com (2026-11-04)
---

1. Get spin kinetic energy at top speed: e.g. 108.8 J at 3150 rpm (E47)(E70).
2. Get peak friction power at top speed: e.g. 132 W small brake (E47) to 264 W with 0.80 N-m disc (E70).
3. Divide energy by disc thermal mass to get rise and check vs pad limit: e.g. 4.35 C rise (E53) -> passes for infrequent competition stops.
4. Retain hold check: if rise is small but hold margin <1.0 vs factored aero 0.72 N-m (E54), increase clamp not mass.
