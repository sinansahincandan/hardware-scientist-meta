1. Set ridge geometry to human-like scale: pitch 0.4-0.5 mm, height 0.3-0.8 mm to amplify stick-slip into 100-500 Hz vibration [E24,E25].
2. Choose skin elastomer for fingertip compliance: Dragon Skin 10A outer 0.8 mm Shore 10A [E21] over softer Ecoflex 00-30 [E20]; check Shore to modulus conversion 0.1-0.5 MPa [E26,E27] and replicate compliance studies [E22,E23].
3. Place transduction under ridges: 28 um PVDF film under ridge layer for dynamic >10 Hz detection [part slip_film], complemented by BioTac-like microvibration principle [E10,E14].
4. Verify slip band: piezoresistive/capacitive static channel <10 Hz for pressure, PVDF/dynamic channel 50-1000 Hz for slip onset [E12,E13].
5. Check: ridge wavelength < taxel pitch/2 to avoid spatial aliasing with 1.5 mm taxels at 2.0-2.5 mm pitch.
