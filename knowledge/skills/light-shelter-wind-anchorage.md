## 1. Check bearing (rarely governs)
1. Compute floor/soil pressure = weight / footprint. Reference: **produce-only floor ~2.45 kPa, plinth soil ~2.51 kPa** for tonne over few m2 [run 20261102-112524_off-grid-solar-powered-cold-storage-for- E47,E48].
2. Compare to **typical allowable soil tens to >50 kPa**; if order below, bearing does not govern [run 20261102-112524_off-grid-solar-powered-cold-storage-for- E43,E44].

## 2. Govern anchoring by wind
3. Compute dynamic pressure **q~0.6*V^2: ~540 Pa at 30 m/s gust** [run 20261102-112524_off-grid-solar-powered-cold-storage-for- E64].
4. Compute wall force `F=q*A`: **~2.86e3 N on ~5 m2 side wall** and carry via anchors, plinth fixings and separate PV supports [run 20261102-112524_off-grid-solar-powered-cold-storage-for- E65].
