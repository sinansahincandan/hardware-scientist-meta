# Insights from Vibration retrofit for a lively pedestrian footbridge
Source run: 20261102-121628_vibration-retrofit-for-a-lively-pedestri

## Footbridge vertical comfort acceleration limits
Setra/HiVoSS vertical serviceability uses acceleration bands around: <0.5 m/s2 maximum comfort, <1.0 m/s2 minimum/mean comfort, >2.5 m/s2 unacceptable. Any footbridge with first vertical mode in the walking step band (~1.5-2.2 Hz) must be checked against these, with dense crowd usually governing.
Evidence: E38 (search: Setra HiVoSS footbridge comfort classes vertical acceleration 0.5 1.0 2.5: WWW.mdpi.com — Experimental and Numerical Study on Dynamics of Two Footbridges with Different Shapes of Girders — https://WWW.mdpi.com/2076-3417/10/13/4505)

## Pedestrian crowd resonant force model
Vertical walking force is modeled as ~700 N body weight with ~280 N (~40%) first harmonic at step resonance. Sparse walkers add linearly, but dense crowd Class III ~0.5 pedestrians/m2 uses imperfect synchronization: equivalent in-phase count Neq ~ 10.8*sqrt(zeta*N) acting together at resonance, producing kN-scale harmonic force and multi-m/s2 bare-deck acceleration when structural damping is ~0.5-1%.
Evidence: E41 (search: Setra footbridge crowd load density synchronized pedestrians equivalent number: mdpi.com — Vibration Serviceability Assessment of a Historic Suspension Footbridge — http://mdpi.com/2075-5309/12/6/732/htm); E42 (search: Setra footbridge crowd load density synchronized pedestrians equivalent number: 1library.net — 4.4.1 Dynamic load cases - Footbridge guide setra — https://1library.net/article/dynamic-load-cases-footbridge-guide-setra.ye693erq); E44 (search: Eurocode pedestrian vertical force harmonic walking frequency: eurocodes.jrc.ec.europa.eu — Microsoft Word - Geradin_JRC_Design of Footbridges_150909.doc — 2021 — https://eurocodes.jrc.ec.europa.eu/sites/default/files/2021-12/jrc_53442.pdf); E49 (equivalent synchronized pedestrians dense crowd: 5.29); E50 (dense crowd resonant force from equivalent pedestrians: 1.48e+03)

## Den Hartog TMD sizing rule for footbridge modes
Vertical tuned mass dampers for footbridge first modes typically use mass ratio mu = mTMD/Mmodal ~1-3%. Den Hartog optimum is frequency ratio fTMD/fstruct = 1/(1+mu), i.e. tuned 2-3% below the structure, and TMD damping ratio ~ sqrt(3*mu/(8*(1+mu)^3)) ~0.09-0.10 at mu=0.025. When tuned and damped near optimum, resonant deck acceleration drops by an order of magnitude (>10x) versus the undamped bridge.

## Low-frequency TMD sag versus dynamic stroke regime
Modal stiffness scales as k = (2*pi*f)^2*Mmodal, e.g. ~2.85 MN/m for 20 tonne at 1.9 Hz. A low-frequency TMD at ~1.85 Hz is soft, e.g. kTMD = (2*pi*fTMD)^2*mTMD ~68 kN/m for 500 kg, so static sag m*g/kTMD is large ~70 mm. Resonant relative stroke under crowd ~20 mm adds to sag, requiring ~90+ mm working spring compression plus over-travel stops.
