## Soft Elastomer Hertz Spread Bridges Discrete Taxels
Millinewton contact on soft silicone is in Hertzian spreading regime, not point-loading. A single-layer elastomer (E~0.3 MPa) spreads 1 mN to ~0.60 mm diameter, insufficient to bridge a 1.5 mm taxel pitch for centroid interpolation. A dual-layer with compliant micropillar base (Eeff ~0.02 MPa, 1.5-2.8 mm total thickness) spreads the same 1 mN to ~1.47 mm, illuminating 2-4 taxels and enabling sub-pitch localization of ~pitch/4 (~0.35 mm) via super-resolution.

## Micropillar Air-Gap Capacitive Transduction Budget at 1 mN
Micropillar air-gap capacitive tactile sensing maps force to capacitance via ~3-4% strain of a 0.5 mm gap. Rule of thumb: baseline capacitance C0 ~40 fF per 1.5x1.5 mm taxel; 1 mN produces ~17-18 um compression (~3.6% strain) giving ΔC ~0.9-1.5 fF. Against a 24-bit CDC (AD7746-class) noise floor of 0.02-0.05 fF this yields SNR 18-30 at threshold, with dual-range solid-elastomer carry-over required to avoid saturation above ~0.5 N to 10-30 N grip.

## Slip Detection Sampling vs Free-Fall Time Budget
Incipient slip detection is constrained by free-fall physics t = sqrt(2d/g). A 1 mm allowable drop corresponds to only 14.3 ms total budget (0.49 mm fallen in 10 ms). Detecting 30-200 Hz stick-slip microvibrations requires burst sampling at 1-2 kHz concurrent with a 200 Hz quasi-static center-of-pressure/stick-ratio track, with flag latency <15 ms to arrest motion before 1 mm excursion.

## Fingertip Envelope, Load and Thermal Limits for In-Hand Sensors
Human fingertip anthropometry imposes a hard envelope ~20x16x13 mm and mass limit ~10-20 g (practical builds achieve ~1.5 g with silicone density ~1.07 g/cm3). Mechanical mount must react 30 N grip (~94 kPa on ~3.2 cm2) and 90 N proof with high margin (e.g., 2x M1.6 screws ~2560 N capacity = ~85x) while preserving gap metrology: fully-supported plate sag ~15.5 um (3.1% of 0.5 mm gap) vs ~384 um as unsupported cantilever. Thermal: silicone k ~0.20 W/mK over 3.2 cm2 with 50-80 mW dissipation yields ~2.5 K conduction rise, keeping skin-safe operation <43C.
