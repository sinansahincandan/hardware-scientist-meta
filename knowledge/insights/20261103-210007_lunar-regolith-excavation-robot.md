# Insights from Lunar regolith excavation robot
Source run: 20261103-210007_lunar-regolith-excavation-robot

## Finding: BP-1 lunar simulant bulk density and friction
Black Point-1 (BP-1) lunar regolith simulant exhibits bulk density ~1.60 g/cc and internal friction angle 39 to 51 deg in cohesionless lab geotechnical characterization. Payload mass for any carried volume scales directly as mass = volume * 1.60 g/cc, e.g., 30 L corresponds to ~48 kg.

## Finding: Vibratory percussion sharply reduces excavation draft
For shallow 10-15 mm peeling with 400 mm wide 35 deg rake blades, percussive/vibratory actuation cuts draft force substantially versus static 70-120 N: ~50% reduction demonstrated in NASA percussion excavation tests and 70% to 93% reduction for longitudinal ultrasonic assistance. Vibrated draft typically falls to 40 to 85 N range at ~25 Hz and ~30 W eccentric drive with 3 mm amplitude.
Evidence: E16 (search: lunar regolith excavation vibratory blade draft reduction: ntrs.nasa.gov — Reducing Extra-Terrestrial Excavation Forces with Percussion — https://ntrs.nasa.gov/api/citations/20120017917/downloads/20120017917.pdf); E19 (search: lunar regolith excavation vibratory blade draft reduction: ascelibrary.org — Ultrasonically Assisted Blade Technologies for Lunar Excavation — https://ascelibrary.org/doi/10.1061/9780784483374.052); E22 (estimate passive cutting force for 20mm depth: 7.28)

## Finding: Steep-wall hopper rule for mass-flow of lunar simulant
Gravity discharge of cohesive BP-1 fines requires hopper walls steeper than the static angle of repose (~45 deg) by a ~15 deg margin to prevent bridging and ensure mass-flow. Practice is >=60 deg walls, UHMW-lined with labyrinth seals, which enables <5 s emptying when pivoted ~45 deg to clear typical 300 mm bin lips.
Evidence: E20 (search: steep hopper angle repose lunar simulant discharge bridging: sciencedirect.com — Vacuum effects on the angle of repose of lunar soil simulants ... — https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0094576525007660); E21 (search: steep hopper angle repose lunar simulant discharge bridging: sciencedirect.com — Flowability of lunar regolith simulants under reduced gravity ... — https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0022489814000251); E25 (skill: steep-hopper-dump-sizing: Use when sizing any steep-wall carry hopper for cohesive bulk solid gravity discharge.)

## Finding: Hub-motor class traction available for soft-soil robots
Commercial 24 V 250 W BLDC geared hub motors (0.8 N.m at 3000 rpm base motor) provide ~35 N.m at the wheel after gearing, or ~233 N per wheel at 0.15 m radius. A 4x4 configuration therefore offers ~933 N peak drawbar, with sustainable traction-to-weight ratios around 0.07 during shallow scraping, well above vibrated draft loads when ground pressure is kept ~10 kPa.

## Finding: Usable energy of 6S semi-solid Li-ion packs
6S 10 Ah 22.2 V semi-solid Li-ion packs (152x60x45 mm class) deliver 222 Wh nominal at ~275 Wh/kg pack (~290 Wh/kg cell) and ~50 A continuous. Usable energy at conventional 85% depth-of-discharge is ~189 Wh, sustaining ~2.1 h at 90 W mixed-mission average (60 W traction + 15 W vibration at 50% duty + 25 W avionics).
