# Insights from Lunar regolith excavation robot
Source run: 20261104-121008_lunar-regolith-excavation-robot

## BP-1 simulant friction and hopper flow regime
Black Point-1 simulant has compacted bulk density near 1500 kg/m3, so 20 L holds ~30 kg, and internal friction 39 to 51 deg with cohesion. Per Jenike mass-flow practice, reliable gravity flow of such cohesive frictional material requires steep smooth wedge walls and a large outlet - e.g. 150 mm diameter gives 0.0177 m2 opening - shallow rough bins arch and stall.
Evidence: E3 (search: Black Point-1 BP-1 lunar simulant bulk density cohesion friction angle: hou.usra.edu — Developing  a New  Space Economy (2019)  5068.pdf Lightning Talk or Poster — 2019 — https://www.hou.usra.edu/meetings/lunarisru2019/pdf/5068.pdf); E7 (search: steep hopper wall angle mass flow cohesive soil Jenike: jenike.com — Case Study - Limestone and Clay Storage Solution | Jenike & Johanson — https://jenike.com/case-studies/reliable-handling-of-limestone-and-clay-at-cemex/); E8 (search: steep hopper wall angle mass flow cohesive soil Jenike: powderbulksolids.com — Powder Perspectives: There is a Science to Powder Handling — https://www.powderbulksolids.com/material-handling-transportation/powder-perspectives-there-is-a-science-to-powder-handling); E10 (hopper payload mass from 20 L volume at BP-1 compacted density: 30 kg); E69 (outlet area for 150 mm dia for arch check: 0.0177)

## Shallow draft scaling in lunar simulant
Shallow scraping at 50 mm depth in loose BP-1 gives draft on order tens of newtons: ~32.9 N for 0.3 m wide blade, scaling approximately with width to ~11 N for 100 mm kerf. At ~1.5 m/s chain speed this is ~16.4 W cutting power, or ~0.5 Wh mechanical energy per 15 kg excavated.
Evidence: E9 (BP-1 shallow scraper cut force estimate for 0.3 m wide blade at 50 mm depth: 32.9 m²⋅Pa); E64 (scale cut force to 100 mm kerf: 11); E59 (ladder chain cutting power from cut force times chain speed: 16.4); E60 (mechanical dig energy per 15 kg trip from ladder power times fill time: 0.512)

## Grouser wheel traction versus ground pressure
Rigid grouser wheels on loose regolith simulant following Bekker pressure-sinkage behavior develop traction far above dig forces when ground pressure is kept low: ~15.6 kPa for a ~46-60 kg class rover on four ~200 mm wheels yields ~294 N available traction versus 11-33 N cutting reaction.
Evidence: E14 (search: skid steer four wheel drive lunar rover grouser wheel regolith traction: robotics.estec.esa.int — Development and Field Testing of Moonraker: a Four-Wheel Rover in Minimal Design — 2014 — http://robotics.estec.esa.int/i-SAIRAS/isairas2014/Data/Session%202a/ISAIRAS_FinalPaper_0114.pdf); E17 (search: Bekker wheel sinkage loose sand ground pressure small rover: 1library.net — Sinkage - Analysis and Experiments - Axel Rover Tethered Dynamics and Motion Planning on Extrem — https://1library.net/article/sinkage-analysis-and-experiments.oz1oxj8q); E23 (stowed envelope sum, ground pressure, tip-over and stopping check: traction 294 N vs cut 32.9N margin 8.9x; stab 206 Nm over 44 Nm SF 4.67; stop dist 62 mm in 0.25 s); E25 (ground pressure four wheels estimate: 1.56×10⁴ kg/m/s²)

## Blind dead-reckoning drift and fiducial reset
Skid-steer wheel odometry plus IMU without external fix drifts to ~0.8 m along-track and ~1.4 m lateral over 40 m, and meters over 60 m. Re-observing a visual fiducial about every 5 m bounds error to 0.1-0.15 m, with 0.03-0.04 m final pose error at 1-2 m range. At 0.5 m/s, link-loss watchdog with 1.0 s latency plus braking needs 0.3-0.6 m stopping distance.
Evidence: E28 (search: skid steer wheel odometry IMU drift localization accuracy: 1library.net — Absolute and relative localization — https://1library.net/article/absolute-relative-localization-description-navigation-processes-cobot-proje.q0j09nvz); E32 (link-loss watchdog stop distance vs latency at 0.5 m/s: T=1.0s coast=0.5 m brake=0.0625 m total=0.562 m; T=2.0s coast=1 m brake=0.0625 m total=1.06 m; final stop x=0.308 m t_stop=0.75 s); E33 (blind localization drift growth odometry+IMU without external fix: dist=40 m along-drift=0.8 m lateral~1.4 m; dist=60 m along-drift=1.2 m lateral~3.14 m; with 5m fiducial reset: bounded error ~0.1-0.15 m); E54 (search: Apriltag fiducial docking accuracy mobile robot alignment: informatik.uni-wuerzburg.de — Advanced Edge Detection of AprilTags for Precise Docking Maneuvers of Mobile Robots — 2022 — https://www.informatik.uni-wuerzburg.de/en/robotics/publications/?tx_extbibsonomycsl_publicationlist%5Baction%5D=download&tx_extbibsonomycsl_publicationlist%5Bcontroller%5D=Document&tx_extbibsonomycsl_publicationlist%5BfileName%5D=ta2022_1.pdf&tx_extbibsonomycsl_publicationlist%5BintraHash%5D=7f39bb4bfa053cc89d3989be7004f178&tx_extbibsonomycsl_publicationlist%5BuserName%5D=nuechter76&cHash=5ca4b90211cc5500565de480df984356)
