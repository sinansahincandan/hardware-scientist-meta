## Low-Reynolds airfoil regime for sub-meter rotors
For chord Reynolds numbers 50k to 150k typical of D ~ 0.4 m HAWTs at 5-11 m/s, SG6043 is characterized as an optimized low-Re airfoil family with usable lift/drag where conventional airfoils stall. At 75% radius Re ~ 50,000 at 5 m/s and ~110,000 at 11 m/s for TSR 5.5-6, flow is in this transitional regime.

## Power and speed scaling of 40-cm class rotors
A rotor with swept area ~0.126 m2 (D=0.40 m) at Cp ~0.30 produces shaft power ~2.9 W at 5 m/s rising to ~30.8 W at 11 m/s. Corresponding speed for TSR 5.5 is ~1310 rpm at 5 m/s to ~2890 rpm at 11 m/s, tip speed ~60 m/s. Rule of thumb: RPM = TSR*V*60/(pi*D).

## Survival thrust and 6061-T6 post capacity
Survival thrust follows T = 0.5*rho*A*V^2*Ct. With Ct up to 1.0-1.3 for a stopped/feathered rotor, T ~37-48 N at 22 m/s for A=0.126 m2. On a 0.4-0.5 m moment arm this gives bending moment ~14.9-19.4 Nm. A 25 mm OD x 2 mm wall 6061-T6 tube with yield ~240 MPa sees stress ~19-25 MPa in this load, giving safety factor ~9.5-12.4 before considering stress concentration.

## Generator voltage constant and electrodynamic dump-brake tradeoff
A BLDC used as generator gives Vdc ~ RPM/KV after rectification, e.g., ~3.6-10.7 V for 1310-2890 rpm with KV 270-360, ~5.8 V with KV500 at 2890 rpm. When braked by a dump resistor, braking torque and time constant are inversely proportional to resistance: for the same inertia/generator, R=6.3 ohm gave tau ~10.8 s and time to <10% speed ~24.8 s with peak ~10 W, while R=2 ohm gave tau ~2.22 s, time to 10% ~5.1 s but peak dissipation ~32.2 W that must be sunk to structure as heat.
