# Insights from Off-grid solar-powered cold storage for smallholder farmers

## Produce cold-storage heat loads
A tonne-scale 4-10 C produce box in ~30 K hot ambient has two distinct energy scales: holding is a few hundred watts continuous (~186 W conduction through ~25 m2 at U~0.22 W/m2K plus respiration, infiltration and fans to ~376 W total), while cooling warm produce from ~30 C to ~5 C needs ~25 kWh thermal per tonne, reflecting ~3.5 kJ/kg-K specific heat. Pull-down therefore dominates daily energy by an order of magnitude over hourly holding and must be budgeted separately in any farm cooler, food-bank fridge, or refrigerated transport.
Evidence: E24 (steady cooling load and pull-down for 1 tonne box: U=0.22 W/m2K A=25.6 m2 Qcond=186 W Qsteady=376 W E_pull=25.0 kilowatt_hour P_pull=1042 watt E_day_elec=3.61 kilowatt_hour); E67 (thermal buffer 1 tonne for 2K rise: 1.94)

## DC vapor-compression plus tropical PV rule of thumb
Small DC vapor-compression coolers operate at electrical COP ~2.5, so ~370-400 W of cold-room load draws ~150 W electric or ~3.6 kWh/day continuous. Tropical fixed-tilt PV yields ~3-4 kWh/day per kWp installed (e.g. ~8.1 kWh/day from 2.4 kWp), so PV must be oversized ~2-3x over holding load to also cover daytime batch cooling, charge losses and cloud margin before battery sizing.

## Battery night autonomy and produce thermal buffer
A 48 V 100 Ah LiFePO4 cabinet stores ~5.1 kWh nominal usable for off-grid cycling. A ~150 W night load for 14 h consumes ~2.1 kWh, about 41% of that store, giving ~1.4 days of holding-only autonomy with no new solar. The stored crop itself adds thermal buffer of ~1.94 kWh per 2 K rise per tonne, useful as passive ride-through for sealed boxes and for setting alarm and backup thresholds in any perishable store.

## Light cold-box ground versus wind loads
A tonne of produce spread over a few square metres imposes only ~2.5 kPa floor and soil pressure, an order of magnitude below typical allowable soil bearing of tens to >50 kPa, so bearing rarely governs lightweight cold stores. Lateral wind governs anchoring: dynamic pressure follows ~0.6 x V^2, or ~540 Pa at 30 m/s gust, yielding ~2.9 kN on a ~5 m2 side wall that must be carried by anchors, plinth fixings and separate PV supports.
