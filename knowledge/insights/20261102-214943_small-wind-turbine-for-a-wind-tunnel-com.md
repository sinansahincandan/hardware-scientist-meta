Source run: 20261102-214943_small-wind-turbine-for-a-wind-tunnel-com

## Wind cube-law power and Betz-Cp ceiling for micro rotors
Shaft power follows P = 0.5*rho*A*V^3*Cp with Cp <= 0.593 Betz limit; practical small 3-bladed horizontal-axis rotors achieve Cp ~0.35 near optimum. At 0.44 m diameter and 11 m/s intercepted power is ~124 W yielding ~43 W shaft. Power scales with V^3 so 5 m/s yields roughly one-tenth the power of 11 m/s, while thrust scales with V^2, making survival loads multiples of operating loads.

## Tip-speed ratio sets rpm and torque scale
Small 3-bladed rotors peak near tip-speed ratio lambda 5-6. Speed follows omega = lambda*V/R. For R=0.22 m, V=11 m/s, lambda=6, omega ~300 rad/s (~2860 rpm). Shaft torque = P/omega is order 0.1 Nm in this class (~0.145 Nm for 43 W), which sizes the generator and defines overspeed thresholds (10% speed ~280-300 rpm).

## Gust thrust and overturning moment sizing for mast-mounted micro turbines
Steady gust thrust on a sub-0.5 m disc at 20+ m/s is order tens of newtons (~45 N at 22 m/s on 0.44 m disc) and produces overturning moment = thrust x hub height above clamp (~13.5 N-m). A thin-wall 6061-T6 aluminum support tube with ~276 MPa yield sees only a few MPa bending stress (~3.6 MPa) in this load class, so static yield is rarely limiting; clamp/post joint capacity, stiffness, vibration and fatigue govern.

## Dump-resistor dynamic braking dynamics and fail-safe stop requirement
Shorting a brushless PM generator through a dump resistor gives approximately exponential spin-down with time constant tau proportional to inertia*R/Ke^2; lower R raises peak power and torque but shortens tau. In the ~40 W, ~300 rad/s class, a few-ohm dump yields tau ~2.2 s, time to 10% = ln(10)*tau ~5.1 s, peak ~10^2 W (324 W at 1 ohm, 162 W at 2 ohm, 65 W at 5 ohm), peak braking torque fractions of a N-m (~0.54 N-m), total dissipated energy ~10^2 J (~180 J). Wind-tunnel competition rules formalize this as fail-safe: fit in 45 cm cube, survive 22 m/s continuously, and on load disconnect or stop command via normally-closed path decay below 10% max rpm within 10 s and stay parked, requiring a separate static friction hold (tens of newtons normal force) exceeding residual aero torque.
