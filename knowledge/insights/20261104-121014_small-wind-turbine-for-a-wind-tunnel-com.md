# Insights from Small wind turbine for a wind-tunnel competition
Source run: 20261104-121014_small-wind-turbine-for-a-wind-tunnel-com

## Sub-meter low-Reynolds rotor power regime
0.4-m diameter rotors in 5-11 m/s winds see ~9.6 W available at 5 m/s rising with V-cubed to ~102 W at 11 m/s. Low-Reynolds sections (SD7080 class) operate near Cp ~0.34 at TSR ~6, giving shaft power ~3.27 W at ~1430 rpm and ~34.7 W at ~3150 rpm.
Evidence: E3 (search: small wind turbine blade airfoil SD7080 SG6043 low Reynolds: repo.lib.jfn.ac.lk — Design of Small Horizontal Axis Wind Turbine for Low Wind Speed Rural Applications — http://repo.lib.jfn.ac.lk/xmlui/handle/123456789/2319); E9 (available wind power at 5 m/s 40cm rotor: 9.62); E10 (available power at 11 m/s 40cm rotor: 102); E11 (shaft power at 5 m/s with Cp 0.34: 3.27); E12 (shaft power at 11 m/s Cp 0.34: 34.7); E13 (rpm at 11 m/s TSR6 40cm rotor: 3.15e+03); E14 (rpm at 5 m/s TSR6: 1.43e+03)

## Outrunner Kv voltage window vs buck-boost input
Rectified voltage from a direct-drive brushless outrunner scales as speed divided by Kv: ~1430 rpm produces ~6.5 V at 220 Kv versus ~8.41 V at 170 Kv, and ~3150 rpm produces ~14.3 V versus ~18.5 V. Off-the-shelf buck-boost modules producing regulated 12 V/3 A accept 8-40 V with ~85% efficiency, so the 170 Kv band keeps the 1430-3150 rpm span inside 8-40 V while the 220 Kv band falls below the 8 V minimum at low wind.
Evidence: E36 (open-circuit volts at top speed Kv220: 14.3); E37 (open-circuit volts at low wind Kv220: 6.5); E76 (required Kv for 8V minimum and revised volts with 170Kv: (179, 8.41, 18.5)); E60 (search: buck-boost DC-DC converter module efficiency 12V datasheet: littlebirdelectronics.com.au — Buck/Boost Converter - 8-40V to 12V/3A | Little Bird Electronics — https://littlebirdelectronics.com.au/products/buck-boost-converter-8-40v-to-12v-3a); E66 (regulated load power at 11 m/s with 85 percent buck-boost efficiency: 22.1)

## Dynamic dump braking cannot hold park
Short-circuit or dump-resistor braking torque declines to zero at zero speed and can be well below survival aerodynamic torque (example 0.155 N-m electrical versus 0.48 N-m aero), permitting runaway to ~9123 rpm when below 315 rpm is required. Dissipation in a 4-ohm grid is ~51 W at ~14.3 V rising to ~85.6 W at ~18.5 V. A spring-applied friction disc above factored aero load (example 0.80 N-m versus 0.72 N-m required, margin 1.67 on 0.48 N-m and 1.11 on factored) arrests ~108.8 J from top speed to below 10% in ~0.673 s and holds 0 rpm at 10 s with disc temperature rise of only ~4.35 K.
Evidence: E48 (electrical-only dump brake fails at 22 m/s - shows need for mechanical: elec-only w@10s rpm 9123 (target <315 rpm) -> FAILS, rotor runs away; elec torque at top Nm 0.155 vs aero Nm 0.48); E70 (Upgraded 0.80 Nm spring disc plus dump stop vs 22 m/s aero torque: t_cross 0.673 s, w10 0.0 rad/s (0.0 rpm); hold margin vs 0.48: 1.67, vs 0.72: 1.11; KE top 108.8 J, peak friction power 264 W); E78 (revised dump power at 18.5V top with 4 ohm: 85.6 W); E53 (brake disc temperature rise from spin energy: 4.35); E65 (search: brushless motor short circuit brake torque zero speed hold: control.com — 3-phase short circuit  BLDC => zero torque — https://control.com/forums/threads/3-phase-short-circuit-bldc-zero-torque.21556/)

## Wind-tunnel competition shutdown and survival rule
Collegiate Wind Competition rules require shutdown to below 10% of maximum speed (example 315 rpm for 3150 rpm top) within 10 s on manual command and on load disconnect, and remaining parked. Survival is assessed at ~22 m/s where dynamic pressure is ~296 Pa, producing ~37.3 N rotor thrust and ~13.1 N-m overturning moment at the mount.
Evidence: E41 (search: Collegiate Wind Competition shutdown brake load disconnect stop requirement: energy.gov — CWC 2019 Rules and Requirements Manual_20190104 — 2019 — https://www.energy.gov/sites/prod/files/2019/01/f58/CWC%202019%20Rules%20and%20Requirements%20Manual_20190104_0.pdf); E75 (10 percent top speed trip threshold: 315 rpm); E51 (dynamic pressure at 22 m/s survival: 296); E22 (survival thrust scale disc load at 22 m/s: 37.3 m²⋅Pa); E23 (overturning moment at post clamp from survival thrust: 13.1 m⋅N)
