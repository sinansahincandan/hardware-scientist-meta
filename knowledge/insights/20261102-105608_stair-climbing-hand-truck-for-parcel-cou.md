## Residential stair geometry regime
Indoor residential stairs cluster around 180-200 mm riser with 250-280 mm going, about 35-39 deg effective slope. Any stair-negotiating carrier must clear a ~200 mm vertical nosing repeated every ~250 mm of tread.

## ISO 11228-2 single-operator push-pull envelope
ISO 11228-2 and HSE guidance limit sustained one-person push-pull to roughly 200-250 N with brief heave below ~350 N using closed-loop grips at ~90-110 cm height. A 60 kg load dragged continuously up a stair slope needs ~441 N, exceeding the envelope; stepwise levered lifts in the ~226 N class or powered assist are needed to comply.

## 6061-T6 thin-wall tube as lightweight bending member
6061-T6 aluminium datasheets give ~240-276 MPa tensile yield. A 30x2 mm round tube weighs ~0.475 kg per m with section modulus ~2.3e3 mm3, so 60-kg-class bending loads run ~30.6 MPa per tube for safety factor ~9 against yield, with small lock pins in double shear at ~36.6 MPa retaining large margin. Aluminium frames with solid tyres can hold tare to ~7-8 kg, under the ~12 kg one-person van lift limit.

## Stair lift energy cost and fail-safe hold torque
Lifting 60 kg through one 0.2 m riser costs ~116-141 J of potential energy. Holding that load plus tare on a stair nosing contact requires ~90-100 N m, sized toward ~198 N m with factor 2 for descent control. Industrial-truck safety practice in the EN 1757 family requires positive locking devices and load restraint with brakes that default to locked when the operator releases control, to prevent runaway on inclines.
