Source run: 20261104-121345_lunar-regolith-excavation-robot

## BP-1 lunar simulant geotechnical regime for earth-test design
BP-1 simulant at Earth gravity presents bulk density 1.50-1.60 g/cm3, angle of repose ~35 deg, friction angle 39-51 deg, and cohesion 0-2 kPa, with fine abrasive particle-size distribution driving IP5X dust sealing. At 1.55 g/cm3 a 45L contained volume equals ~70 kg, so batch mass quickly exceeds small-rover mass.

## Shallow drag-scraper draft vs wheeled traction rule of thumb
For a plane-blade shallow cut, Balovnev/McKyes predicts draft ~8.4 N cohesionless to ~48 N at 2 kPa cohesion for 400 mm wide x 25 mm deep cut in BP-1. Against ~294 N traction for a 60 kg vehicle at mu=0.5 (589 N weight), traction/draft margin is 6x to 35x, allowing traction-limited excavation without high-power drums if depth is kept 20-30 mm.

## Rear-pivot high-lift dump stability and clearance limits
Rear-pivot dumping stability is governed by restoring moment about the rear axle vs overturning moment of payload behind the axle; example 243 Nm restoring vs 49.1 Nm for 20 kg at 0.25 m gives 4.95x margin. Clearing a 300 mm fixed bin lip requires sizing tilt to ~55 deg for pour point ~0.76 m, and steep walls >50 deg (>35 deg repose) are needed to avoid bridging of BP-1 in gravity discharge.

## LiFePO4 component class thermal and usable-energy limits
LiFePO4 class: discharge -20 to 60 C, charge 0 to 45 C, requiring conduction to chassis via sealed bay for thermal control. Usable energy is ~60% of nameplate with 20-80% SoC window, e.g., 480 Wh nameplate -> 288 Wh usable -> ~1.92 h at 150 W average, defining sortie duration without facility power.

## Teleoperation link-loss fail-safe stop distance standard
Standard for out-of-sight operation requires link-loss detection and drive cut within 0.5 s via DDS heartbeat + independent watchdog. Stop distance = latency distance + braking distance; at 0.3 m/s, 0.5 s delay, 1.0 m/s2 braking this is ~0.195 m (~1 wheel diameter for 200 mm wheel), which sizes speed and braking for remote latency.
