# Insights from Lunar regolith excavation robot
Source run: 20261102-153907_lunar-regolith-excavation-robot

## BP-1 simulant is high-friction cohesionless soil
Treat Black Point-1 lunar simulant as cohesionless frictional granular media with high internal friction angle and negligible cohesion. Shear strength is normal-pressure dependent, not cohesive, so cutting and traction forces scale with overburden, cut geometry, and friction angle. The fines are angular and abrasive with high dust ingress risk to mechanisms and electronics.

## Loose-soil pressure and drawbar margin
Wheeled mobility on loose simulant scales with nominal ground pressure = weight / total contact area, e.g. ~589 N on four 150x100 mm patches is ~9.8 kPa. Usable drawbar pull is a small fraction of weight; a sustained horizontal tool load of tens of newtons is order 0.06-0.07 weight ratio and consumes roughly half the available friction-limited margin. Use Bekker pressure-sinkage plus grouser shear for sinkage and slip, not paved-tire friction.

## COTS LiFePO4 pack specific energy limits
Purchasable 24 V LiFePO4 packs deliver ~90-120 Wh/kg at pack level including cells, BMS, wiring and case, e.g. 480 Wh in ~5.3 kg is 90.6 Wh/kg and ~819 Wh in 6.8 kg is ~120 Wh/kg. A 0.5 h sortie at ~341 W needs ~170 Wh, so a 480 Wh pack gives ~1.4 h full-load runtime. Pack claims above ~150-200 Wh/kg, e.g. 1000 Wh in 2.92 kg at 342 Wh/kg, are not LiFePO4 pack reality and indicate cell-only or erroneous numbers.

## IP65 sealing and latency-limited teleoperation
IP65 for dusty field electronics means dust-tight with no harmful dust ingress plus protection against water jets from any direction, realized with gasketed sealed box, sealed connectors and cable glands. Non-line-of-sight driving over 5 GHz WiFi FPV has ~100 ms-class video plus control latency, giving order 0.25 m stopping distance even at crawl speed, so the link needs loss-of-heartbeat stop-and-hold. Budget Jetson Orin Nano-class teleoperation overhead at order 29 W and ~1.7 kg for compute, cameras, depth sensing and radio.
