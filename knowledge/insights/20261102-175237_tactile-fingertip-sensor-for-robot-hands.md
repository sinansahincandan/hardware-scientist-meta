Source run: 20261102-175237_tactile-fingertip-sensor-for-robot-hands

## Human fingertip size and tactile acuity
Human fingertip domes are ~16 mm diameter class modeled as soft ellipsoids under compression; static two-point discrimination is 1-2 mm. Artificial touch approaching human localization operates in this mm-scale spatial sampling regime.

## Soft skin elastomer compliance regime
Platinum-cure silicones in Shore A 10-20 range provide fingertip-like compliance in 1-6 mm thick stacks with sub-mm fingerprint texture. Contact stress at 1 mN threshold over a 1 mm-radius patch is ~0.32 mN/mm2, defining the low-end pressure regime for soft touch transduction.
Evidence: E5 (search: Dragon Skin silicone Smooth-On Shore hardness datasheet: smooth-on.com — Dragon Skin™ FX-Pro™ vs. Dragon Skin™ 20 NV — https://www.smooth-on.com/compare/40768/1014/a/); E6 (search: Dragon Skin silicone Smooth-On Shore hardness datasheet: smooth-on.com.cn — Dragon Skin ™ FX Pro — 2018 — http://www.smooth-on.com.cn/uploadfile/2018/0506/20180506090051442.pdf); E7 (estimate contact pressure at 1 mN threshold over 1 mm radius patch: 0.318 mN/mm²)

## Capacitive taxel baseline and CDC resolution
Sub-mm parallel-plate taxels with ~200 um gaps have baseline capacitance of order tens of fF, e.g. ~26.1 fF. Capacitance-to-digital converters with 16 pF full scale and 16-bit conversion provide ~0.244 fF LSB with femtofarad noise floors, placing millinewton normal steps near the quantization limit.
Evidence: E10 (search: capacitance to digital converter resolution femtofarad noise AD7147 datasheet: datasheet.sisoog.com — AD7147 Datasheet - sisoog — https://datasheet.sisoog.com/AD7147/file/zmedia/dex/a5fb8d30243eab589beed344bdcf3376_AD7147.pdf); E15 (CDC LSB from 16pF span over 16 bits: 0.244 fF); E17 (baseline taxel capacitance parallel plate: 26.1 fF)

## Slip vibration bandwidth and reaction time
Incipient slip appears as shear-field divergence across the contact plus vibration energy in the few-hundred-Hz Pacinian FA-II band. Single-channel sampling at 1 kHz provides 500 Hz Nyquist coverage of that band, and sense-to-grip-command loops close on the order of tens of milliseconds, e.g. ~34 ms budget.
