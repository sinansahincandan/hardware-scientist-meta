# Insights from Off-grid solar-powered cold storage for smallholder farmers

## Off-grid PV yield and battery sizing rule
Usable off-grid PV energy follows kWh/day ~= kWp x worst-month peak-sun-hours x ~0.75 system derate. At 5.0 PSH this is ~9.0 kWh/day for 2.4 kWp and ~13.5 kWh/day for 3.6 kWp. Overnight refrigeration needs dispatchable electricity: 48V LiFePO4 via MPPT sized for night cooling load plus cloudy-day reserve, with charge current ~= kWp*1000/48, e.g. ~75A for 3.6 kWp.
Evidence: E14 (daily PV yield kWh for 2.4kWp at 5 PSH: 9); E83 (daily yield for 3.6kWp grown array: 13.5); E93 (required peak kWp for reconciled electrical load: 2.81); E95 (24h battery SoC normal sun and one cloudy day with surge and shed: cloudy pv 4.05 end 3.73 min 3.73; night 18-6 load 3.02 kWh; surge check done); E97 (MPPT charge current amps for 3.6kWp at 48V: 75)

## Hot-ambient vapour-compression lift and COP
Holding 5C interior at 43C ambient is ~38K lift with condensing near 55C. Small DC vapour-compression R134a monoblock / BD-class compressors deliver COP ~1.4 under those hot conditions, so ~12.8-13.5 kWh_thermal/day converts to ~10.4-10.7 kWh_electrical/day plus evaporator fans and controls. Thermal-only storage cannot run fans, controls, or compressor start surge.
Evidence: E102 (search: Secop BD50F datasheet COP R134a evaporating temperature: darment18-test.sprintit.fi — BD50F Direct Current Compressor R134a, 12-24V DC & 100-240V AC 50/60Hz — https://darment18-test.sprintit.fi/web/content/77489?download=true); E109 (single thermal total conduction respiration door pulldown fans: 13.5); E111 (reconciled electrical total with curtain latent door model: 10.4); E112 (search: "BD50F" COP 1.4 condensing 55C evaporating: files.danfoss.com — MAKING MODERN LIVING POSSIBLE — https://files.danfoss.com/download/CorporateCommunication/DanfossCity/Compressors-for-extreme-conditions.pdf); E108 (electrical daily total from thermal divided by COP plus fans controls: 10.7)

## Produce cold-store thermal load hierarchy
Produce cold-store heat is dominated by field-heat pull-down: 1000 kg from 30C to 5C is ~25 kWh sensible; staged 250 kg/day is ~6.2-6.25 kWh/day, ~260W average pull power within ~615W total average and ~9-10 h pull time. For reference, conduction through ~25.4 m2 of 100-120mm PIR at 38K delta is ~203W continuous, and door infiltration with strip curtain and ~8 brief single-crate openings is ~0.17 kWh/day sensible plus latent versus ~0.44 kWh/day for 4 full air changes without control.
Evidence: E25 (sensible pull-down kWh for 1 tonne produce 30C to 5C: 25); E28 (revised steady load with 120mm PU and staged loading pull-down: 250kg/day avg pull power=260 W energy=6.2 kWh; total avg with pull=615 W; 250kg batch pull time 9.7 h); E41 (conduction watts at 43C ambient 5C room: 203); E57 (door infiltration kWh per day for 4 air changes: 0.44); E110 (single door model 8 curtain openings sensible plus latent: 0.169); E109 (single thermal total conduction respiration door pulldown fans: 13.5)

## Crate, mass, soil-pressure and wind-uplift magnitudes
One tonne at ergonomic 20 kg per 600x400mm vented crate is 50 crates requiring ~8.6 m3 interior with two-row racking and air gaps for rear-high to low-return circulation. A 400W crystalline module footprint is ~1.95 m2, so multi-kW arrays are ~12-18 m2. Complete box plus racking plus array is tonne-class, e.g. ~2.2 t total, giving pad pressures ~18-23 kPa, well below ~150 kPa soft-soil allowable but still requiring spread pads. Wind load follows q ~= 0.6*V^2, ~375 Pa at 25 m/s gust, producing hundreds of kg uplift on array and box, e.g. ~673 kg on ~17.6 m2, requiring distributed ground screw anchors of kN-class each with field pull test.
