## Human fingertip and distal phalanx envelope sets size budget for wearable/robotic fingertip devices
Human distal phalanx is ~16.8 mm long and the fingertip envelope is ~18-22 mm long x 15-18 mm wide x ~15 mm high. This bounds available volume and datum for any device that must mount to an anthropomorphic finger stump.

## Effective modulus and Hertz spread of soft silicone skins at millinewton loads
Solid silicone with ~0.3 MPa modulus spreads a 1 mN normal touch to ~0.60 mm diameter with mean pressure ~3570 Pa. Introducing a micropillar air-gap with ~30% fill can lower effective modulus to ~0.02 MPa (using Ecoflex 00-30 / Dragon Skin 10A class materials), spreading the same 1 mN to ~1.47 mm diameter at ~586 Pa (~0.586 mN/mm2). Total structured stack thickness ~1.5 mm occupies ~1382 mm3 / ~1.5 g silicone.

## Capacitive tactile taxel baseline and fF-level signal vs CDC noise floor
A ~2.25 mm2 (1.5x1.5 mm) parallel-plate taxel with sub-mm air-gap dielectric has baseline capacitance C0 ~39.8 fF. A 1 mN compression producing ~2.2% gap strain yields Delta-C ~0.88 fF. 24-bit capacitance-to-digital converters of the AD7746/AD7147 class provide ±4 pF to 17 pF input range with ~0.02 fF noise, giving ~44x margin for 1 mN detection and covering the 5 N range via distributed loading.

## Slip micro-vibration band, ridge scale, and tactile data/bend limits
Human-like fingerprint ridges 0.30 mm high at 0.50 mm pitch transduce slip into 60-700 Hz micro-vibrations detectable by 28 um PVDF films sampled >=1 kHz. A 30-taxel array sampled at 500 Hz with 16 bit/sample generates 240 kbps, fitting within 400 kHz / 1 MHz I2C. Polyimide flexible circuits carrying such arrays require bend radius >3 mm for reliability and can operate at ~45 mW / <0.05 W thermal budget.
